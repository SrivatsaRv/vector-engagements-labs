# Code of conduct

Vector Engagement Labs is an engineering and research community. Participation requires professional, evidence-based, and respectful conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss models, code, data, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- State uncertainty, provenance, limitations, and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome correction and distinguish disagreement from misconduct.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, responsible disclosure, and applicable law.
- Avoid operational targeting requests, harassment, threats, discrimination, or glorification of real-world harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, intimidation, sexualized conduct, deliberate misinformation, undisclosed plagiarism, attempts to bypass project safety boundaries, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when behavior threatens people or the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact documented in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will minimize disclosure and avoid conflicts of interest when handling a report.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community calls, and public representation of the project.
