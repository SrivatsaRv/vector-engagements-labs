Status: issue #178 establishes value-free verification tooling only. Parent issue #142 remains open, and no table value is admitted until the complete two-entry comparison and page-grounded adjudication gates pass.

## Authority, scope, and nonclaims

NASA-TP-1538 is the only numeric authority for this corpus. The frozen official PDF has SHA-256 `aae0ece64474291368c0b4c816d3ab327c6100329e6eb030c2f4545d0913feb3`; the frozen NTRS metadata has SHA-256 `bc15d569ade8a40525f87c1e1fb6970a03dde6c241d55fcefd3351ba061e2a89`; and the source manifest has SHA-256 `d4736dae888054e502c34912374b8c032dd52f84414bc7e9137b9953acbe4e6b`. The NTRS decision is `PUBLIC` / `GOV_PUBLIC_USE_PERMITTED`, contains no identified third-party material, and records `NO` for export, EAR, and ITAR controls.

The subject is exactly `NASA_GENERIC_F16` and the deployment class is exactly `ENGINE_VERIFICATION_ONLY`. This corpus is low-speed subscale wind-tunnel evidence. It does not represent a PAF F-16 Block 52, a production aircraft model, propulsion, flight-control laws, altitude or Reynolds corrections, aeroelasticity, tactics, weapons employment, or mission outcomes. It cannot satisfy production model-pack admission.

## Complete source and table inventory

The frozen page surface is PDF pages 8–12 for symbols, units, and axes; 42–46 for Appendix B equations; 49 for Table I; 51–97 for every Table III table; and 100 for the body-axis figure. Table III contains 48 logical tables and 14,705 declared positions. The earlier page-85 truncation is invalid.

The closed axes are `alpha_deg = [-20,-15,-10,-5,0,5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45,50,55,60,70,80,90]`, `beta_deg = [-30,-25,-20,-15,-10,-8,-6,-4,-2,0,2,4,6,8,10,15,20,25,30]`, longitudinal stabilator planes `[-25,-10,0,10,25] deg`, lateral stabilator planes `[-25,0,25] deg`, and deep-stall stabilator planes `[-25,-10,0,10,15,20,25] deg`. Tables printed only through alpha 45° retain the same canonical alpha axis and mark every 50°–90° position `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`; they do not create a parallel short alpha axis.

The inventory is `CX` pages 51–57, `CZ` pages 58–64, `Cm` pages 65–73, `CY` pages 74–79, `Cn` pages 80–88, and `Cl` pages 89–97. It includes every base, leading-edge-flap, speed-brake, aileron, rudder, pitch/yaw/roll-rate, beta, stabilator-effectiveness, alpha increment, and deep-stall increment printed in those pages. Blank cells are `PRINTED_BLANK`; an unreadable mark is `ILLEGIBLE`; a coordinate outside a table's declared axes is `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`. None is zero.

One printed-table/equation boundary is explicit rather than silently guessed. Table III prints the one-dimensional `delta-Cn-delta-a(alpha)` table on PDF page 87, but the Appendix B total-`Cn` equation and its definitions on PDF page 45 do not reference that table. `CN_AILERON_INCREMENT` therefore remains fully transcribed and independently evaluable by exact lookup/interpolation, but its assembly decision is `LOOKUP_ONLY_WITHHELD_FROM_ASSEMBLY`. Appendix B's published two-dimensional `Cn` aileron surfaces remain in the aileron assembly term. A successor source decision is required before the withheld one-dimensional table can affect a total coefficient.

Body axes are +X forward, +Y right, and +Z down. Positive stabilator is nose-down; positive aileron/differential-tail command is left roll; positive rudder command is left yaw; and positive leading-edge flap is down. Table I's printed SI and customary values remain paired source facts rather than conversion-generated substitutes.

## Independent transcription and adjudication protocol

Entry A and entry B use separate worktrees, branches, agents, task contexts, entrant identities, and isolation-session identifiers. Each entrant receives only the frozen source manifest, lossless source crops, the empty exact-coordinate template, and these instructions. Neither receives the other transcript, its digest, a comparator report, OCR numeric output, third-party numeric files, or an adjudication record. Repository search, network lookup, PDF text-layer extraction, copy/paste from a numeric derivative, and shared scratch files are prohibited during entry. OCR may help locate a heading but cannot populate a cell.

Each completed transcript contains all 14,705 coordinate decisions in canonical table/axis order and is content-addressed. `scripts/freeze-tp1538-transcription.mjs` admits a bounded, complete draft and writes one no-overwrite, read-only artifact whose filename is the canonical transcript digest; it then performs exact readback without modifying the entry draft. The deterministic comparator runs only after both complete transcripts are frozen. It reports every exact printed-string mismatch, state mismatch, missing or duplicate coordinate, ordering difference, and identity conflict, while rejecting reports above the closed two-transcript structural maximum of 29,410 mismatches. It does not emit agreements, silently normalize decimal spelling, or repair data.

A third adjudication context receives only the comparator output, frozen source pages/crops, the empty coordinate inventory, and this protocol—not either full raw transcript. The comparator writes one canonical-digest-named, no-overwrite, read-only report and verifies exact readback. Exact agreements become `AGREED_DOUBLE_ENTRY`; every mismatch is re-read from the cited source crop and receives a separate immutable `SOURCE_ADJUDICATED` decision with adjudicator identity, page, coordinate, chosen state/value, and rationale. Unresolved cells remain explicit and prevent corpus admission. Final assembly verifies the two frozen transcript digests and decision digest, then binds raw A, raw B, the comparison, decisions, and every admitted cell lineage into one versioned corpus manifest. The finalizer likewise writes the corpus under its canonical content digest, makes it read-only, reports the distinct raw-byte digest, and performs exact readback before reporting success.

Transcription A remains outside any branch or location visible to the B entrant until B is frozen. A tooling commit may be shared only if it contains the empty template and no A values, progress counts, digests, comparator output, or hints. No partial pull request may represent this protocol as complete.

## Evaluator and immutable identity

The single schema owner in `scripts/lib/tp1538-aero-corpus.mjs` owns exact source, page, table, configuration, coordinate, availability, transcript, comparison, adjudication, and corpus admission. It generates the static Rust inventory; Rust does not maintain a parallel table or axis schema. Only after full corpus admission may the TypeScript adapter project the exact 14,705 resolved cell states/values into `vector.tp1538-aero-evaluator-batch.v1`. The separate verification Rust/WASM evaluator validates that complete projection and independently executes lookup, interpolation, and Appendix B assembly. Its result is accepted only after complete TypeScript replay against the admitted corpus.

All requests and results bind the exact source-manifest, transcript, comparison, decision, corpus, evaluator-contract, verification-model-pack, and backend identities. `vector.tp1538-aero-verification-record.v1` is the read-only VSR/evidence envelope: bounded UTF-8 JSON readback replays every lookup and preserves the exact corpus and verification-model-pack digest. It is not `EngineRun`, `SimulationEventV2`, or a production VSR extension. The production engine and simulation Worker bundles must contain none of the corpus or evaluator authority. A separate verification-only Worker admits the corpus once, bounds jobs to 4,096 operations, fails closed without initialization, survives rejected jobs, and can be recreated from the same immutable corpus after termination.

The evaluator does not extrapolate. Symmetry is used only where the publication explicitly supplies it and a positive and negative control prove that exact rule. Caller mutation, reordered axes, swapped pages, partial tables, forged recomputed digests, and wrong units, signs, configurations, subject, or deployment class fail closed before lookup.

## Verification and performance

Completion evidence must include exhaustive schema rejection; independent source-grounded knot and interior oracles; increment, derivative, configuration, symmetry, assembly-order, boundary, missing-cell, mutation, persistence/readback, VSR identity, TypeScript/Rust-WASM full-state parity, Worker recovery, production-import and built-bundle isolation, memory/capacity, WASM size, deterministic performance, `make ci-local`, and exact-SHA clean-clone gates.

The pre-optimization performance contract is fixed as follows and may not be relaxed to create a pass: raw corpus bytes at most 32 MiB; each transcript at most 8 MiB; one complete corpus admission p95 at most 30,000 ms; an admitted TypeScript evaluator's governed 4,096-operation exact/interior lookup batch p95 at most 100 ms; the equivalent Node-hosted Rust/WASM batch p95 at most 500 ms; verification Worker initialization/recovery at most 30,000 ms and its admitted 4,096-operation round trip p95 at most 1,000 ms; retained RSS growth at most 256 MiB; and generated verification WASM below 500,000 bytes. `scripts/lib/tp1538-aero-performance-evidence.mjs` is the executable owner of those fixed limits. It rejects a dirty or non-SHA repository before measurement, requires an exact 4,096-operation workload and configuration mix bound by both raw-byte and canonical-content digests, validates every measured batch against the frozen complete-result digest outside timed work, and retains all samples and output sizes. The final immutable workload must bind its corpus, request/result, case-count, configuration-mix, output-byte, expected-digest, runtime, CPU, memory, commit, and clean-worktree identities. These are verification workload limits, not browser simulation, named-aircraft, or product-capacity claims.
