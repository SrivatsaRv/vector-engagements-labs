## Complete source and table inventory

The frozen page surface is PDF pages 8–12 for symbols, units, and axes; 42–46 for Appendix B equations; 49 for Table I; 51–97 for every Table III table; and 100 for the body-axis figure. Table III contains 48 logical tables and 14,705 declared positions. The earlier page-85 truncation is invalid.

The closed axes are `alpha_deg = [-20,-15,-10,-5,0,5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45,50,55,60,70,80,90]`, `beta_deg = [-30,-25,-20,-15,-10,-8,-6,-4,-2,0,2,4,6,8,10,15,20,25,30]`, longitudinal stabilator planes `[-25,-10,0,10,25] deg`, lateral stabilator planes `[-25,0,25] deg`, and deep-stall stabilator planes `[-25,-10,0,10,15,20,25] deg`. Tables printed only through alpha 45° retain the same canonical alpha axis and mark every 50°–90° position `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`; they do not create a parallel short alpha axis.

The inventory is `CX` pages 51–57, `CZ` pages 58–64, `Cm` pages 65–73, `CY` pages 74–79, `Cn` pages 80–88, and `Cl` pages 89–97. It includes every base, leading-edge-flap, speed-brake, aileron, rudder, pitch/yaw/roll-rate, beta, stabilator-effectiveness, alpha increment, and deep-stall increment printed in those pages. Blank cells are `PRINTED_BLANK`; an unreadable mark is `ILLEGIBLE`; a coordinate outside a table's declared axes is `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`. None is zero.

One printed-table/equation boundary is explicit rather than silently guessed. Table III prints the one-dimensional `delta-Cn-delta-a(alpha)` table on PDF page 87, but the Appendix B total-`Cn` equation and its definitions on PDF page 45 do not reference that table. `CN_AILERON_INCREMENT` therefore remains fully transcribed and independently evaluable by exact lookup/interpolation, but its assembly decision is `LOOKUP_ONLY_WITHHELD_FROM_ASSEMBLY`. Appendix B's published two-dimensional `Cn` aileron surfaces remain in the aileron assembly term. A successor source decision is required before the withheld one-dimensional table can affect a total coefficient.

Body axes are +X forward, +Y right, and +Z down. Positive stabilator is nose-down; positive aileron/differential-tail command is left roll; positive rudder command is left yaw; and positive leading-edge flap is down. Table I's printed SI and customary values remain paired source facts rather than conversion-generated substitutes.

The adjudication contract computes one canonical coordinate-inventory digest from these exact axes and 48 table descriptors. Every draft and frozen decision artifact binds that digest, so a reordered axis, changed table/page descriptor, missing coordinate, duplicate coordinate or extra decision cannot be presented as the inventory reviewed by the comparator. The completed corpus resolves 13,587 positions as `AVAILABLE`, 37 as `PRINTED_BLANK`, one as `ILLEGIBLE`, and 1,080 as `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`.

## Independent transcription and adjudication protocol

The completed workflow preserves entry A content SHA-256 `b081618e2667fb2a4032c5795fde7a59ecd5a3d762a8cf8dffc90ce8afd049a3`, entry B `c4818c918c3ab71e23108e7368664f5c0f8db39e0eb148994e8b93e5052e6de8`, comparison content `49ee3b022da4d6b0bd570a02fda181b24ac22a28e03d4a5221986ada89299ccf`, comparison raw bytes `2f0e3083e554a80646c3e2c1ba1230aa777637ab1933bc4ea5f77273bf44ae60`, and frozen adjudication `6cdd364686b1eb28c082878157e504e628718fc34ee821510501dea4e2a145a7`. The comparison contains 13,394 exact agreements and 1,311 resolved mismatches with zero unresolved positions.

Entry A and entry B use separate worktrees, branches, agents, task contexts, entrant identities, and isolation-session identifiers. Each entrant receives only the frozen source manifest, lossless source crops, the empty exact-coordinate template, and these instructions. Neither receives the other transcript, its digest, a comparator report, OCR numeric output, third-party numeric files, or an adjudication record. Repository search, network lookup, PDF text-layer extraction, copy/paste from a numeric derivative, and shared scratch files are prohibited during entry. OCR may help locate a heading but cannot populate a cell.

Each completed transcript contains all 14,705 coordinate decisions in canonical table/axis order and is content-addressed. `scripts/freeze-tp1538-transcription.mjs` admits a bounded, complete draft and writes one no-overwrite, read-only artifact whose filename is the canonical transcript digest; it then performs exact readback without modifying the entry draft. The deterministic comparator runs only after both complete transcripts are frozen. It reports every exact printed-string mismatch, state mismatch, missing or duplicate coordinate, ordering difference, and identity conflict, while rejecting reports above the closed two-transcript structural maximum of 29,410 mismatches. It does not emit agreements, silently normalize decimal spelling, or repair data.

A third adjudication context receives only the comparator output, frozen source pages/crops, the empty coordinate inventory, and this protocol—not either full raw transcript. The comparator writes one canonical-digest-named, no-overwrite, read-only report and verifies exact readback. Exact agreements become `AGREED_DOUBLE_ENTRY`; every mismatch is re-read from the cited source crop and receives a separate immutable `SOURCE_ADJUDICATED` decision with adjudicator identity, page, coordinate, chosen state/value, and rationale. Unresolved cells remain explicit and prevent corpus admission. Final assembly verifies the two frozen transcript digests and the complete frozen adjudication artifact, then binds raw A, raw B, the comparison canonical and raw-byte identities, the adjudication content digest and decisions, and every admitted cell lineage into one versioned corpus manifest. The finalizer likewise writes the corpus under its canonical content digest, makes it read-only, reports the distinct raw-byte digest, and performs exact readback before reporting success.

`scripts/manage-tp1538-adjudication.mjs` is the only decision-entry path. It accepts a bounded, regular, non-symbolic-link comparison whose read-only filename equals its canonical digest. `create` emits a `0600` empty `vector.tp1538-adjudication-decisions.v1` draft that binds the comparison raw-byte and canonical digests, source-manifest digest, compiled coordinate-inventory digest, A/B transcription IDs, adjudicator ID, and every mismatch coordinate and required PDF page in comparator order. `apply` accepts exactly one closed `SOURCE_READ` JSON decision, never exposes either full transcript, and rejects a second write to the same coordinate. `validate` succeeds only when every and only mismatch slot is resolved once. `freeze` leaves the draft unchanged and exclusively creates `<contentSha256>.json` at mode `0444`, then verifies its raw bytes and canonical content by exact readback. The corpus finalizer requires and embeds that frozen decision artifact, including its content digest and comparison raw/canonical binding; an ungoverned array is not admissible and two raw-distinct comparison/adjudication chains cannot collapse to one corpus identity.

The offline command sequence is:

```sh
node scripts/manage-tp1538-adjudication.mjs create --comparison /absolute/path/<comparison-sha256>.json --output /absolute/path/decision-draft.json --adjudicator-id <distinct-id>
node scripts/manage-tp1538-adjudication.mjs apply --comparison /absolute/path/<comparison-sha256>.json --draft /absolute/path/decision-draft.json --decision-json '<closed-source-read-decision-json>'
node scripts/manage-tp1538-adjudication.mjs validate --comparison /absolute/path/<comparison-sha256>.json --input /absolute/path/decision-draft.json
node scripts/manage-tp1538-adjudication.mjs freeze --comparison /absolute/path/<comparison-sha256>.json --input /absolute/path/decision-draft.json --output-directory /absolute/path/frozen-decisions
```

Each `--decision-json` object has exactly `tableId`, `coordinate`, `pdfPage`, `decision`, `chosenState`, `chosenPrintedValue`, and `rationale`; `decision` is exactly `SOURCE_READ`. `AVAILABLE` requires a finite base-10 printed decimal string. `PRINTED_BLANK` and `ILLEGIBLE` require a null value. The coordinate and page must identify the same comparator mismatch and governed source page. Rationale and adjudicator identifiers are bounded, and the finalizer rejects an adjudicator who is either entrant.

Transcription A remains outside any branch or location visible to the B entrant until B is frozen. A tooling commit may be shared only if it contains the empty template and no A values, progress counts, digests, comparator output, or hints. No partial pull request may represent this protocol as complete.

## Evaluator and immutable identity

The single schema owner in `scripts/lib/tp1538-aero-corpus.mjs` owns exact source, page, table, configuration, coordinate, availability, transcript, comparison, adjudication, and corpus admission. It generates the static Rust inventory; Rust does not maintain a parallel table or axis schema. The admitted artifact is `governance/nasa-tp1538-generic-f16-aero-verification-corpus.v1.json`. The TypeScript adapter projects its exact 14,705 resolved cell states/values into `vector.tp1538-aero-evaluator-batch.v1`. The separate verification Rust/WASM evaluator validates that complete projection and independently executes lookup, interpolation, and Appendix B assembly. Its result is accepted only after complete TypeScript replay against the admitted corpus.

All requests and results bind the exact source-manifest, transcript, comparison, decision, corpus, evaluator-contract, verification-model-pack, and backend identities. `vector.tp1538-aero-verification-record.v1` is the read-only VSR/evidence envelope: bounded UTF-8 JSON readback replays every lookup and preserves the exact corpus and verification-model-pack digest. It is not `EngineRun`, `SimulationEventV2`, or a production VSR extension. The production engine and simulation Worker bundles must contain none of the corpus or evaluator authority. A separate verification-only Worker admits the corpus once, bounds jobs to 4,096 operations, fails closed without initialization, survives rejected jobs, and can be recreated from the same immutable corpus after termination.

The evaluator does not extrapolate. Symmetry is used only where the publication explicitly supplies it and a positive and negative control prove that exact rule. Caller mutation, reordered axes, swapped pages, partial tables, forged recomputed digests, and wrong units, signs, configurations, subject, or deployment class fail closed before lookup.

Before corpus creation, the finalizer parses the frozen decision artifact against the exact immutable comparison raw and canonical identities and verifies its digest-named read-only file. Corpus admission then embeds and replays the complete frozen artifact, requires its `FROZEN` state and content digest, checks its comparison raw/canonical and A/B bindings against the embedded comparison, and derives resolved cells only from its validated `SOURCE_READ` slots. Projecting a bare decision array is not an admissible path. This changes no lookup, interpolation, Appendix B assembly, Rust/WASM ABI, Worker message or production-runtime behavior.
